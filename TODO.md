# ZanHR Lab Website Outcomes

## Mission-led homepage
- [ ] Present ZanHR Lab as an Africa-focused Human Resource Management education platform.
- [ ] Explain the HR education focus and the Knowledge → Trust → Community → Reputation → Clients → Company philosophy.

## Audience pathways
- [ ] Provide clear pathways for students, job seekers, employees, managers, entrepreneurs, and organizations.
- [ ] Include distinct calls to action for individuals and organizations.

## Practical HR education
- [ ] Cover HRM fundamentals, recruitment, employment rights, workplace behavior, labour relations, and HR laws in a practical resources section.
- [ ] Use a content-ready layout that can support future SEO articles and educational guides.

## Career and workplace resources
- [ ] Present career development resources for CV improvement, interview preparation, LinkedIn development, and career planning.
- [ ] Present workplace and organization resources covering HR practices, policies, recruitment, employee engagement, and workplace systems.

## Credibility and growth
- [ ] Highlight integrity, learning, practicality, accessibility, innovation, and professionalism.
- [ ] Position future career services, HR consulting, corporate training, recruitment support, analytics, and HR technology as planned growth areas.

## Growth pathways and delivery
- [ ] Include newsletter growth and contact pathways for individuals and organizations.
- [ ] Include responsive, accessible navigation and footer links for YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, LinkedIn, and Facebook.
